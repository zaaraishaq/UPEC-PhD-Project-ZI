# UPEC Genomic Research Portal

A research portal collecting three connected studies on uropathogenic *Escherichia coli* (UPEC) — covering a single clinical genome, a vaccine designed from its core proteome, and a machine-learning model trained on over a thousand genomes worldwide[cite: 1].

Deployed live at: [https://zaaraishaq.github.io/UPEC-ML-data/](https://zaaraishaq.github.io/UPEC-ML-data/)[cite: 1]

---

## Overview & Research Arc

This portal highlights a continuous research line developed within the **Integrative Biology Laboratory (IBG)** at the Atta-ur-Rahman School of Applied Biosciences (**ASAB**), **NUST**, under the supervision of **Prof. Amjad Ali**[cite: 1]:

1. **One Strain, One City (2025):** Whole-genome sequencing and comparative pangenome analysis of U1, a multidrug-resistant clinical isolate from Rawalpindi, placing it within the high-risk ST131 / O25:H4 / B2 lineage[cite: 1].
2. **From Genome to Vaccine (2023):** Reverse vaccinology across 307 global UPEC genomes to narrow a 2,977-protein core proteome down to a 216-residue poly-epitope construct evaluated against TLR2 and TLR4[cite: 1].
3. **One Model, 1,184 Genomes (2026):** An interpretable machine learning framework (Random Forest + SHAP) spanning 1,184 genomes and 75,069 features to prioritize 20 candidate biomarkers of lineage success[cite: 1].
