How the work developed

Each study answers a question the last one raised — from what makes one strain resistant, to what a global model can predict about the lineage it belongs to.

research lineage, 2023 – 2026 · not drawn to genetic scale
2025 — Antonie van Leeuwenhoek

One strain, one city

Whole-genome sequencing of U1, a multidrug-resistant clinical isolate from Rawalpindi, set against 73 Pakistani UPEC genomes. Identified U1 as ST131 / O25:H4 / phylogroup B2 — the same high-risk lineage circulating globally — and mapped its resistance genes, virulence factors and mobile elements.

2023 — J. Biomolecular Structure & Dynamics

From genome to vaccine

Reverse vaccinology across 307 global UPEC genomes narrowed a 2,977-protein core proteome to three outer-membrane candidates. Nine B-cell–derived T-cell epitopes were assembled into a 216-residue poly-epitope construct and evaluated computationally against TLR2 and TLR4.

2026 — Frontiers in Bioinformatics (in review)

One model, 1,184 genomes

A comparative genomics and interpretable machine-learning framework spanning 17 dominant sequence types and over 12,000 statistically significant markers, interpreted with SHAP, to prioritize 20 candidate biomarkers of lineage success — including ampC, feoB, shuT and hutX.

Genomic characterization

Genomic characterization and comparative analysis of multidrug-resistant uropathogenic Escherichia coli strain U1 from Pakistan revealed ST131 as dominant clade

Antonie van Leeuwenhoek · 119, 22 · 2026

Sequences and characterizes U1, an MDR UPEC strain from a confirmed UTI case in Rawalpindi, then places it within a 73-genome comparative pangenome of Pakistani UPEC isolates. U1 carries blaCTX-M-15 and dfrA17 among 71 predicted resistance genes, 35 virulence factors and four plasmid replicon types, and clusters tightly with the globally disseminated ST131 lineage.

Vaccine design

Immunoinformatics aided designing of a next generation poly-epitope vaccine against uropathogenic Escherichia coli to combat urinary tract infections

Journal of Biomolecular Structure and Dynamics · 2023

Applies pan-genomics and reverse vaccinology to 307 UPEC genomes to identify three conserved, surface-exposed vaccine candidates (PhoE, CusC_1, OmpF). Nine prioritized B-cell–derived T-cell epitopes were fused with a cholera toxin B adjuvant into a single 216-residue construct, which showed favorable docking and stable molecular-dynamics behavior against TLR2 and TLR4 over 120 ns simulations.

Machine learning

Prioritizing lineage-specific genomic features in uropathogenic Escherichia coli using comparative genomics and interpretable machine learning framework

Frontiers in Bioinformatics · in review · manuscript 1765766

Integrates pangenomic, resistome, virulome and mobile-element data for 1,184 global UPEC genomes into a 75,069-feature matrix. A Random Forest classifier, interpreted with SHAP, resolves 17 dominant sequence types (led by ST131 at 39.1% and ST1193 at 16.1%) and prioritizes a 20-marker biomarker panel — including the ampC beta-lactamase and the iron-acquisition genes feoB, hutX and shuT — as candidates for future genomic surveillance.

1,184 genomes 176 sequence types 75,069 features 20 SHAP biomarkers Random Forest · 98.8% holdout accuracy
